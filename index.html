GHOSTVERSE Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

GHOSTVERSE is a Discord bot that provides server moderation, welcome messages and related community features.

2. Information Processed

Depending on enabled features, the bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames, member join and leave events, and messages or moderation information needed to operate its features.

3. Use of Information

Information is used to operate bot features, support moderation, prevent spam and abuse, and troubleshoot errors.

4. Storage and Sharing

Information may be stored in files, databases or logs if configured. We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared when necessary to operate the service, address security issues or comply with applicable law.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as needed for the bot's purposes. Contact us to ask questions or request deletion.

6. Your Choices

Server administrators can remove the bot from their server. Users may contact us with privacy questions.
